retain the selected R1 opening; 21–25 seconds retain the first 120 R2 bottle
frames. The other 77 seconds are new Isaac Sim 6.2.0-rc.14 captures. The lower
eyeline mouth/strap insert replaces the first three seconds of the new oddity
take; only its remaining nine seconds are selected. Per-shot casts comprise
24 actor IDs across the film, rather than 24 continuously instantiated actors
in every shot. The source-frame map is authoritative, including unused ranges

The film preserves the local-rotation, fixed-anatomy pipeline. Healthy escape
uses a supported brake and route choice; the red interceptor commits to an
old target, misses, overruns and recovers. The door shot remains ten seconds
of one uninterrupted source take. The later route stays outside the passage
until the bench threat is resolved. The companion returns to a separated
help position; all three survivors cross the gate plane before it closes.
Slow actors use the actual SDK slow-zombie cache and staggered stimuli.

Each new run records actual USD roots, full skeletons, long-bone ranges,
rendered leaf instance IDs, actor visibility, camera poses, RGB hashes and
source ticks. The recorder advances a fixed 1/30-second step and waits for
render; camera warmup steps advance zero simulation time. Clip assembly uses
the corresponding source frames without changing their authored duration.
The graded masters fade to black over the final 0.25 seconds. The silent
source assembly remains ungraded and unfaded.

Selected static-box probes and selected 10 Hz deformed-surface checks are
documented under evidence. They do not establish whole-film continuous
collision clearance, self-collision, contact forces or physical support.
The stance audit covers declared door support windows only. Failed capture,
camera, blocking and geometry iterations remain separate, without promotion.

for footsteps, muffled car steps, bottle/ring/fabric/metal transients and a
sparse pulse score. The PA uses original Mandarin text rendered by the locally
installed eSpeak NG. No cloned speaker or transit recording is used.
